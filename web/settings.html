🔗 Cross-Platform Syndication & Outro CTAs

Automatically cross-promote your content across YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram. Cross-link your published videos in descriptions and comments, and append platform-tailored call-to-action outros.

🎬 End-of-Video Outro Cards (Automated FFmpeg Stitching)

Appends high-conversion platform-specific outro cards at the end of every video before upload:

  • YouTube: "Like, Share & Subscribe" with channel name and subscription badge
  • Facebook: "Like, Share & Follow Page" with your page badge
  • Instagram: "Like, Comment & Follow" with your @handle badge
🎨 Custom Video Outro Support (Clipchamp, Premiere, CapCut):
Prefer your own animated outro? Create a 3–5s clip on Clipchamp or your video editor and place the MP4 file in: data/outros/

If a custom MP4 is present in data/outros/outro_<platform>.mp4, OmniCast automatically rescales and concatenates it instead of the generated card!

🌐 Cross-Platform Links in Descriptions & Comments

Drive traffic between platforms. Once a video is published, OmniCast injects companion links directly into the YouTube description and posts pinned/top comments on Facebook and Instagram.

⚡ 7-15 Day Video Optimizer Operating Window

Checking Window…

The video optimizer audits videos that have been live for 7–15 days and auto-pivots underperforming titles and thumbnails. To avoid disturbing viewers and triggering algorithmic penalties at night, video details are only updated during the daytime window.

When enabled, audits underperforming videos during daytime hours and rotates titles/thumbnails automatically with rate-limited pacing.

💾 Backup & Restore

Export the full database (schedules, channels, connections, video library) as a .db file. Import a backup to restore all settings. The app will save an automatic pre-restore backup before overwriting.
