🔌 Connections

All external services are managed here. YouTube and Gemini accounts are completely independent, so different Google accounts can be used.

📺 YouTube Accounts

Each connected account has its own client_secret.json, credentials, channel mapping, folder and scheduler.

Add YouTube Account

Give this OAuth client a clear name, choose its client_secret.json, then connect with Google. Reconnecting a logged-out account keeps its existing channel, folder, inventory and scheduler data.

Select YouTube Channel

Choose which channel from this Google account should be stored.

📘 Meta — Facebook + Instagram

Connect your Facebook Page and linked Instagram Professional account for publishing.

Connect Meta Account

Enter your Meta App details and Facebook Login for Business Configuration ID.

Select Facebook Page

Choose the Facebook Page this uploader should use.

✨ Gemini

Separate Google OAuth connection used for direct video understanding and English/Roman Hinglish metadata generation. Multiple Gemini accounts are supported.

Add Gemini Account

Each Gemini account can use its own Google OAuth client_secret.json. Give it a clear name, choose the JSON file, then sign in with the Google account you want to use.

Google Cloud: Enable the Generative Language API in the project shown by your OAuth client. If the OAuth app is in Testing mode, add each Gemini Google account under Test Users.
